The Problem

Pain Points

  • ~45 minutes per page to manually write accessibility bug tickets
  • Tickets lack remediation guidance — developers need follow-up
  • No deduplication — same violations filed repeatedly
  • Inconsistent severity ratings across QA engineers
  • Only 8–12 pages audited per sprint

Business Risk

  • E-commerce sites face ADA/Section 508 legal liability
  • A single checkout button failure blocks thousands of users
  • Accessibility backlogs grow because triage is tedious
  • Demand letters triggered by missed violations
Definition of Success: Reduce per-page triage from ~45 min to <5 min. Generate bug reports with correct WCAG criteria (≥90%) and code-level fix suggestions that developers can act on without follow-up.

Bug Report Detail: A11Y-002

CRITICAL — A11Y-002
TitleMissing Image Alt Text on Product Images
WCAG1.1.1 — Non-text Content (Level A)
Pageproduct-listing.html
Selectorimg[src$="shoe-red.jpg"]
ImpactBlocks blind users from understanding products
ReporterA11Y Auto-Triage Bot
Labelsaccessibility images

Steps to Reproduce

  1. Load the product listing page
  2. Inspect images with src 'shoe-red.jpg' and 'shoe-green.jpg'
  3. Verify images lack alt attributes

Affected Users

Blind users (screen readers), low vision users, cognitive disabilities, slow connections

Suggested Fix

<img src="shoe-red.jpg"
     alt="Red running shoe, side view"
     width="200" height="200">
Why Critical: A blind user on this page hears "image, shoe-red.jpg" from their screen reader — they cannot identify the product, compare options, or make a purchase decision. This blocks the entire shopping flow for 2.3% of the population.

Recommendation & Next Steps

Ask: Pilot on 3 e-commerce pages next sprint. Success metric: 80%+ tickets closed without developer follow-up questions.

What It Replaces

  • Manual axe-core scan → copy to spreadsheet
  • Write bug ticket from scratch (45 min)
