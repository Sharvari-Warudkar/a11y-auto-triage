# WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 1.4.3: Contrast (Minimum) (Level AA)

## Requirement
The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, except for:
- Large text: at least 3:1 (large text is 18pt or 14pt bold)
- Incidental: text that is part of inactive UI, pure decoration, not visible, or part of a picture
- Logotypes: text that is part of a logo or brand name has no contrast requirement

## Who is affected
- Users with low vision
- Users with color vision deficiency
- Older users with declining contrast sensitivity
- Users viewing screens in bright sunlight or glare conditions

## Common failures
- Light gray text on white backgrounds (#aaa on #fff = 2.3:1, fails)
- Placeholder text with insufficient contrast
- Error messages with low contrast (e.g., light red on white)
- Disabled-looking but actually active elements
- Text over images without sufficient overlay

## How to measure
- Contrast ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) where L1 is lighter luminance
- Tools: WebAIM Contrast Checker, Chrome DevTools color picker, axe-core
- Test against the actual computed background, not just the parent element

## Sufficient techniques
- G18: Ensuring that a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 exists between text and background
- G145: Ensuring that a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 exists for large text

## Remediation
1. Increase text darkness or lighten background to achieve 4.5:1 ratio
2. Use a contrast checker tool to verify all text colors against their backgrounds
3. For prices, stock status, and other important info: never go below #595959 on white (#fff)
4. Consider providing a high-contrast mode toggle for users who need more than AA
5. Test with the page zoomed to 200% — contrast must still pass at all sizes
