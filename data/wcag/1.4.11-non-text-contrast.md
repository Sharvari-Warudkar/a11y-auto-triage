# WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 1.4.11: Non-text Contrast (Level AA)

## Requirement
The visual presentation of the following have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against adjacent colors:
- UI Components: Visual information required to identify user interface components and states (e.g., form field borders, button boundaries)
- Graphical Objects: Parts of graphics required to understand the content (e.g., chart elements, icons)

## Who is affected
- Users with low vision (approximately 4% of the population)
- Users with color vision deficiency
- Older adults with declining contrast sensitivity

## Common failures
- Form field borders with insufficient contrast against page background
- Icon-only buttons where the icon has low contrast
- Focus indicators that don't meet 3:1 contrast
- Custom checkboxes or radio buttons with light borders
- Chart data points or lines that are indistinguishable from background

## Sufficient techniques
- G195: Using an author-supplied, highly visible focus indicator
- G207: Ensuring that a contrast ratio of 3:1 is provided for icons
- G209: Provide sufficient contrast at the boundaries between adjoining colors

## Remediation
1. Ensure all form field borders are at least 3:1 against their background
2. Test interactive component states (hover, focus, active, disabled) for contrast
3. Use borders or backgrounds on buttons — not just text color
4. For icons that convey meaning, ensure 3:1 contrast against adjacent colors
5. Add visible focus indicators with sufficient contrast (not just outline: none)
